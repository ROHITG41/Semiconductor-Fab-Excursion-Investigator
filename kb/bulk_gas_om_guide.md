# Bulk gas operations guide (synthetic project knowledge base)
Source type: synthetic guidance written for this prototype. Not a SEMI standard and not vendor documentation.

## GAS-01 Moisture rising in bulk nitrogen
Compare moisture at the tank outlet, the purifier outlet and the point of use. If only the readings at and after the purifier rise, the purifier is the likely source. If the tank outlet is also high, look upstream at the supply.

## GAS-02 Purifier bed saturation
A purifier bed near the end of its rated capacity lets moisture through. Check purifier runtime against its rated life. Corrective action: regenerate or replace the bed, then verify moisture at the purifier outlet and the point of use.
