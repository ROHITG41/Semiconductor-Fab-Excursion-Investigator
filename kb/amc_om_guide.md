# Airborne molecular contamination guide (synthetic project knowledge base)
Source type: synthetic guidance written for this prototype. Not a SEMI standard and not vendor documentation.

## AMC-01 AMC rising in a process bay
Compare AMC at the make-up air inlet with AMC in the bay. If the inlet is steady while the bay rises, the chemical filtration stage is the likely source.

## AMC-02 Chemical filter breakthrough
Chemical filter media loads up over time. Near the end of rated life, contaminants break through into the supply air. Check filter runtime against its rated life and the differential pressure history. Corrective action: replace the filter media and verify AMC in the bay afterwards.
